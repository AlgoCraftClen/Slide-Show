# EXECUTIVE EVALUATION SUMMARY
**Project:** TCPA-2026-JB-001 (107,000 Jute Sacks)
**Date:** May 7, 2026
**Subject:** Technical Evaluation & Recommendation for Award

## 1. Overview
The Authority has completed the public bid opening and preliminary technical review of four (4) submissions. The evaluation is conducted under the **L2 Weighted Scale**, prioritizing "Value for Money" by balancing **Financial** competitiveness (60%) with Technical, Delivery, and Reliability factors (40%).

## 2. Bidder Performance Summary
- **AGRIX Asia:** The price leader ($159,430). Fully committed to the 1.2m length and purple stripe. Only concern is a minor administrative resubmission on the day of the opening.
- **Adachi Corporation:** The most detailed technical proposal. They identified a container weight error in the original RFB, proving high reliability. However, they are the most expensive ($300,894).
- **Infinity Supply:** Quoted for only **105,000 sacks** (2,000 short) and a length of 47 inches (short of the 1.2m requirement).
- **Holland Commodities:** Significant technical deviations. Quoted **Blue Stripes** (wrong **color**), **44-inch length** (8cm too short), and **105,000 units** (short quantity).
